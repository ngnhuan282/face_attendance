1. Tong quan du an

He thong diem danh sinh vien bang nhan dien khuon mat la ung dung web xay dung tren Django, cho phep giang vien diem danh tu dong thong qua webcam. He thong nhan dien khuon mat sinh vien realtime, ghi nhan ket qua vao co so du lieu va tao bao cao chuyen can theo tung lop hoc phan.
Tinh nang chinh:
Nhan dien khuon mat realtime qua webcam (face_recognition + OpenCV)
Quan ly sinh vien, hoc phan, lop hoc phan theo mo hinh truong dai hoc
Giang vien chon lop va bat dau diem danh chi voi vai thao tac
Canh bao tu dong khi sinh vien vang > 20% tong so buoi
Xuat bao cao Excel mau sac truc quan

2. Cac module he thong

He thong gom 8 Django app va 1 module AI doc lap. Cac app duoc phan cong cho tung thanh vien de lam viec song song hieu qua.

Can viet va migrate theo dung thu tu sau de tranh loi ForeignKey:
Buoc 1: academics/models.py — Faculty, Department, AcademicYear, Semester
Buoc 2: accounts/models.py — Teacher (lien ket voi Department)
Buoc 3: students/models.py — Student (lien ket voi Department, Semester)
Buoc 4: courses/models.py — Course, CourseClass (lien ket voi Semester, Teacher)
Buoc 5: schedules/models.py — Schedule (lien ket voi CourseClass)
Buoc 6: attendance/models.py — AttendanceSession, AttendanceRecord
Buoc 7: notifications/models.py — Notification (lien ket voi Student, CourseClass)
